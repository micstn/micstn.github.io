# Design System & Aesthetic Instructions: "PRISMARY"

Adopt a meticulous, architectural blueprint and technical drafting aesthetic for this site. The overall vibe should feel like an engineer's technical manual, patent drawing, or precision scientific notebook.

## 1. Typography & Naming
* **Site Name:** PRISMARY
* **Logotype/Brand Style:** Uppercase, wide letter-spacing (tracking), structured, elegant serif or clean high-contrast geometric font (resembling a technical stamp or classic drafting font).
* **Body Font:** Clean, readable technical sans-serif or monospaced font for code/metadata, paired with a crisp serif for body text.

## 2. Color Palette & Canvas
* **Background:** Clean off-white, parchment, or blueprint light-paper tone (e.g., `#F9F9F8` or a very faint cool grid-paper gray `#F4F5F6`). Avoid pure stark white.
* **Primary Lines & Text:** Deep charcoal or blueprint ink (e.g., `#1A1A1A` or dark navy/slate `#2C3E50`).
* **Accents:** Minimal. Use precise hairline borders (`1px` solid borders in light gray/charcoal) and subtle technical crosshairs or dimension lines where appropriate.

## 3. Logo Treatment (Upper Left Corner)
* **Asset:** Use a small, high-contrast monochrome version of the technical prism logo (the hexagonal prism with refraction arrows and dotted alignment/extension lines).
* **Placement:** Fixed or aligned strictly to the upper-left corner of the header/navigation bar.
* **Dimensions:** Rendered small and crisp (e.g., height: 28px–36px) next to the word "PRISMARY" or acting as the home link.

## 4. UI Components & Layout Guidelines
* **Borders & Dividers:** Use thin, sharp architectural lines (`border: 1px solid #d1d5db`) instead of modern soft drop-shadows. Content should feel "drawn" onto the grid.
* **Navigation:** Minimalist, text-only, separated by vertical drafting lines or slashes (e.g., `Index / Essays / About`).
* **Content Cards / Sections:** Enclose text blocks or essay previews in clean, thin-lined bounding boxes that mimic technical diagrams or layout grids.
